Data & Account Deletion

You can delete your LlamaQueue account and the associated application data stored for that account. This action cannot be undone.

What deletion does

  • Stops your connected Twitch and YouTube chat workers.
  • Attempts to revoke saved Twitch and Google chat authorization tokens.
  • Deletes your LlamaQueue account, connected-account records, profile data, queue data, settings, moderator records, history, cached chat identities, and other user-owned database records.
  • Clears active in-memory chat messages and disconnects active dashboard/overlay sockets for the account.

Account deletion does not delete content already held by YouTube, Twitch, Nightbot, or other platforms. If a provider revocation request cannot be completed, revoke LlamaQueue's access directly in that provider's security settings. Some operational logs or backup copies may remain until their normal retention cycle expires.
