Privacy Policy

LlamaQueue is an independently operated software project that provides queue-management, unified livestream chat, moderation, and related creator tools. It is not operated by a registered company or corporation. For privacy questions or requests, contact Rjmortii@gmail.com.

1. Services and third parties

LlamaQueue can connect to Google and YouTube, Twitch, and Nightbot when you choose to use those integrations. LlamaQueue uses YouTube API Services. YouTube is not affiliated with or endorsing LlamaQueue. The service is hosted using Render infrastructure. Each third-party service processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: provider account identifiers, email address, display name, profile image, and linked channel identifiers, names, handles, and thumbnails returned during sign-in or account connection.
  • Authorization information: OAuth access and refresh tokens and token-expiration information needed to maintain integrations. LlamaQueue does not ask for or store your Google, Twitch, or Nightbot account password. Chat integration tokens are encrypted in the application database using a key derived from the server-side SESSION_SECRET. Nightbot credentials are also encrypted before storage in the current version.
  • YouTube API Data: channel and live-broadcast metadata, live-chat messages, message identifiers and timestamps, author channel identifiers and display names, profile images, moderator/owner/verified flags, membership information, badges, membership tenure, Super Chat details, and gifting events when provided by YouTube and needed for chat features.
  • Twitch and Nightbot data: account and channel identifiers, display names, chat messages and event details, badges, subscription or moderation information, and command information required by the enabled integration.
  • Queue and creator data: queue registrations, player/display names and identifiers entered by users, queue state, lobby history, moderator names and permissions, settings, statistics, and appearance preferences.
  • Chat preferences and identity cache: platform user identifiers, usernames, membership/subscription flags, tiers or tenure where available, overlay tokens, and chat appearance preferences.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts; connect the services you select; receive and display chat; send messages or moderation actions that you initiate; display channel and live-stream information; manage queues, registrations, moderators and settings; maintain connection recovery; protect the service; and respond to support or deletion requests. YouTube API Data is used only to provide or improve LlamaQueue features visible to the authorizing user. It is not sold, used for advertising, or provided to data brokers.

4. Where information is displayed or shared

Information is processed by LlamaQueue and its hosting infrastructure, and sent to Google/YouTube, Twitch, or Nightbot as needed to perform the integration you enable. Your connected chat may be shown in your LlamaQueue dashboard and in the OBS/browser overlay associated with your account. Anyone with an overlay URL you choose to share may be able to view that overlay. LlamaQueue does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers. We do not currently integrate third-party advertising or analytics services.

5. Storage and retention

Account, queue, settings, moderator, history, and integration records are stored in the application's database on its hosting environment. OAuth credentials are stored for as long as needed to maintain the connection, and are encrypted in the current application version. Live chat message content is held in a limited in-memory rolling buffer for the active chat session (up to 300 recent messages per LlamaQueue account); it is not written to the application's persistent chat-history database by the unified chat receiver. Chat identity and membership metadata used for priority and display may be stored in the database until the integration is disconnected, the account is deleted, or the data is otherwise no longer needed. Browser preferences such as language, pinned chatters, sidebar state, and bot filters may be stored in localStorage. A signed session cookie is used for login/session continuity, and the service worker caches static application assets.

We retain information only as needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may delete your account through the Data & Account Deletion page. When YouTube authorization is revoked or cannot be refreshed, associated YouTube API Data is deleted as soon as practicable and within the period required by YouTube's policies. Some minimal records may remain temporarily in infrastructure backups or operational logs until those systems' retention cycles expire.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect Twitch and YouTube chat integrations from the chat page. Disconnecting removes the saved chat connection and associated platform chat identity/resume data and requests token revocation from the provider where supported. Basic account/profile fields used to sign in to LlamaQueue may remain while the LlamaQueue account itself remains active. You can revoke LlamaQueue's Google access at Google Account security permissions. You may also request deletion of your LlamaQueue account and associated database records at /data-deletion, or email Rjmortii@gmail.com if you cannot sign in. Account deletion attempts to revoke saved Google and Twitch chat tokens and removes local account data. If a provider is unavailable, you may need to revoke access directly in that provider's account settings.

7. Cookies and local storage

LlamaQueue uses a signed session cookie for authentication and session continuity. The interface stores selected preferences in browser localStorage and caches static assets through a service worker. These technologies support the service and its user interface; the current application does not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect account and integration data, including encrypted storage for OAuth integration tokens and authenticated sessions. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your account and overlay links private, and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

9. Children

LlamaQueue is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when features, data practices, or legal requirements change. If a material change affects Google user data practices, users will be asked to review and accept the updated policy before the changed practices are used. Questions or privacy complaints: Rjmortii@gmail.com.

11. YouTube and Google disclosures

LlamaQueue's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. LlamaQueue uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
